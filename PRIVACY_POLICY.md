# Privacy Policy

**Presentation Note**  
**Effective date: October 13, 2026**

Presentation Note is a desktop application by Vu Nguyen. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

## Information the app handles

Presentation Note does not require an account and does not collect or send your information to the developer. The app does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

The app stores the information you enter, such as presentation titles, cases, steps, notes, and related presentation content, in local JSON files. By default, these files are stored in `Documents\Presentation Note`. You can choose a different folder in the app's settings.

The app also stores preferences locally. These may include your selected storage folder, keyboard shortcuts, overlay position for each display, overlay opacity, and collapsed-overlay corner. The app reads display information to position and size its presentation overlay; it does not capture your screen. Configured keyboard shortcuts work globally while the app is running so you can control the presentation, but the app does not record your general keystrokes.

If you choose to import or export presentation data, the app reads or writes a JSON file at the location you select. Exported files and copies you make are under your control.

## How information is used and shared

Local content and preferences are used only to provide the app's features. Presentation Note does not transmit this information to the developer or other parties. The app does not include an online service or cloud synchronization.

The app does not control information that Windows or other software may process independently under your device settings.

## Retention and deletion

Your information remains on your device until you delete it. To remove it, close the app and delete `steps.json` from the configured storage folder and `settings.json` from `Documents\Presentation Note`. Also delete any exported files or backups you want removed. If you chose a custom storage folder, deleting the app alone may not remove files in that folder.

## Security

Your presentation files are stored as local JSON files and are not encrypted by Presentation Note. Protect access to your device and any folders or exported files containing information you consider private.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's data practices change. The effective date above indicates when this version took effect. The latest version will be included with the app or published with the project.

## Contact

For questions about this policy or the app's privacy practices, contact the developer through the [Presentation Note project page](https://github.com/vunguyen96/presentation-note). Please do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.
